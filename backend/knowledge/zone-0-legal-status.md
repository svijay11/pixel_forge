The Legislature passed AB 3074 (2020) and SB 504 (2024), making changes to Public Resource Code 4291. These changes require the Board of Forestry and Fire Protection to establish an ember-resistant defensible zone the first 5 feet around a structure, referred to as Zone 0.

Governor Newsom’s Executive Order N-18-25 requires that the Board complete rulemaking for Zone 0 no later than December 31, 2025.

Ready for Wildfire (defensible space page) states Zone 0 is not legally required yet, while describing it as key for wildfire defense.
