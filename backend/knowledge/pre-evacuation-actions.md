Pre-evacuation actions from Ready for Wildfire:

1. Keep fire extinguishers in your home, and train your family on their use. Regularly inspect them for proper functionality and expiration dates.
2. Make sure everyone in your household knows where your gas, electric, and water main shut-off controls are located and how to safely operate them in an emergency.
3. Prepare an Emergency Supply Kit for each family member, as recommended by the American Red Cross, containing essentials for immediate needs.
4. Store an extra Emergency Supply Kit in your vehicle to ensure access to essential supplies if you can’t reach your home due to a wildfire.
5. Maintain a list of emergency contacts, easily accessible both in your home and within your emergency supply kits.
6. Use a portable radio or scanner to stay informed about the current wildfire situation for timely decision-making during emergencies.
7. Share the Ready, Set, Go! initiative and your Wildfire Action Plan with your neighbors to promote community-wide wildfire preparedness.
